## Method and limits

- Volume is total units at budget gross profit per unit; mix is the residual, so the four parts reconcile exactly rather than approximately.
- Gross profit only: no overhead allocation, no tax, no foreign exchange, no write-offs. This is a trading pack, not a P&L.
- The ledger is synthetic and seeded; the fingerprint proves which ledger produced these numbers.
